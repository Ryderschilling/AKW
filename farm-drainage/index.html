The part most contractors skip

GPS field tile install and mapping

Tile only works if the grade is right for the whole run. AKW installs with GPS machine control, which holds the planned grade foot by foot instead of trusting an eyeball and a laser pole. No bellies in the line, no backfall, no dead runs you find out about next spring.

Every job is mapped as it goes in. You get an as-built record of where every main and lateral sits, how deep, and what size. When you repair a line in ten years, add a wet spot to the system, or sell the farm, that map is worth real money.

Questions

What people ask before they call.

What is GPS tile mapping?

While the tile goes in, the GPS records exactly where each main and lateral runs and how deep it sits. You get that as-built map when the job is done, so you know where your tile is before you dig, repair, add on, or sell the ground.

Why install tile with GPS grade control?

Tile has to fall the whole way to the outlet. GPS machine control holds the planned grade foot by foot through the pull, which keeps low spots and backfall out of the line so the water keeps moving.

When should I get on the schedule for fall tile?

Field tile goes in after harvest, roughly October until the ground freezes, plus early spring. The fall schedule fills up in August, so the earlier you call, the better your spot.

Do you tile a single wet spot or only whole fields?

Both. A targeted run for one wet spot is a common job, and so is pattern tiling a whole field. Kevin will walk it with you and tell you what makes sense for that ground.
