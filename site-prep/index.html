Site prep and excavating

Dug right, so the next crew is not fixing it.

A crooked basement dig or a pad that is off grade becomes the concrete crew’s problem, then the builder’s, then the owner’s. Getting the dirt work right at the start is cheaper than every fix after it.

AKW digs basements and footings, grades pads, driveways and final grade, sets culverts, and clears ground with a forestry mulcher. Builders, contractors and homeowners use them because the site is ready when it is supposed to be.

Work runs across the area around Brimfield, including Brimfield, Elmwood, Farmington, Princeville, Hanna City, Yates City, Williamsfield, Dunlap, Kickapoo, Peoria, Eureka, Metamora, Washington, Canton, Knoxville and Galesburg.

What we do on a job site

Dirt work for builders and homeowners.

Basements and footings

Dug to depth, corners squared and footings marked for the concrete crew.

Building pads

Pads cut and built up to the grade the plans call for.

Final grade

Ground shaped so water runs away from the house and the yard is ready for seed.

Driveways and culverts

Driveways cut in and culverts set so the water crosses where it should.

Land clearing

Trees and brush taken out along fence lines, field edges and building sites.

Forestry mulching

A mulcher head grinds brush and small timber in place. No burn pile left behind.

Questions

What people ask before they call.

Do you dig basements for new houses?

Yes. AKW digs basements and footings, squares the corners and marks the footings for the concrete crew.

Do you work for builders or homeowners?

Both. Builders and contractors use AKW for site prep, and homeowners call for basements, driveways, grading and clearing.

What is forestry mulching?

A mulcher head on the excavator grinds brush and small trees into mulch right where they stand. The ground gets cleared without a burn pile or a pile of logs to haul.

Can you set a culvert under my driveway?

Yes. Driveways and culverts are part of the site prep work AKW does.

Is the site visit free?

Yes. The site visit and the estimate are free and there is no obligation.

What area do you cover?

AKW works out of Brimfield, Illinois and covers Peoria, Knox, Fulton, Stark and Woodford counties, including Brimfield, Elmwood, Farmington, Princeville, Hanna City, Yates City, Williamsfield, Dunlap, Kickapoo, Peoria, Eureka, Metamora, Washington, Canton, Knoxville and Galesburg.
