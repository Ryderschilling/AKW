Ponds

A pond should hold water, not cattails.

Ponds fill in. Silt washes in off the ground above them, cattails move in along the edge, and a few years later the deep end is not deep and the bank is sliding in.

AKW digs new ponds and brings old ones back. The long-reach excavator pulls silt and cattails out of the water without tearing up the bank you want to keep, and the spoil gets hauled or spread where you want it.

Landowners and homeowners call from all over the area, including Brimfield, Elmwood, Farmington, Princeville, Hanna City, Yates City, Williamsfield, Dunlap, Kickapoo, Peoria, Eureka, Metamora, Washington, Canton, Knoxville and Galesburg.

Questions

What people ask before they call.

Can you clean out a pond without draining it?

Often, yes. A long-reach excavator can pull silt and cattails from the bank while the water stays in. Whether that works for your pond depends on its size and depth, which Kevin will tell you after he walks it.

What happens to the silt and muck you dig out?

It gets hauled off or spread and graded on your property, whichever you prefer. That gets settled before the work starts.

Do you build new ponds?

Yes. AKW digs new ponds and shapes the banks and the spoil around them.

Can you fix a bank that keeps washing out?

Yes. Banks get cut back to a stable slope, and rock can be set along the waterline where the waves hit.

Is the site visit free?

Yes. The site visit and the estimate are free and there is no obligation.

What area do you cover?

AKW works out of Brimfield, Illinois and covers Peoria, Knox, Fulton, Stark and Woodford counties, including Brimfield, Elmwood, Farmington, Princeville, Hanna City, Yates City, Williamsfield, Dunlap, Kickapoo, Peoria, Eureka, Metamora, Washington, Canton, Knoxville and Galesburg.
