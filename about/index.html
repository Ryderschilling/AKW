The part most contractors skip

GPS field tile install and mapping

Tile only works if the grade is right for the whole run. AKW installs with GPS machine control, which holds the planned grade foot by foot instead of trusting an eyeball and a laser pole. No bellies in the line, no backfall, no dead runs you find out about next spring.

Every job is also mapped as it goes in. You get an as-built record of where every main and lateral sits, how deep, and what size. When you repair a line in ten years, add a wet spot to the system, or sell the farm, that map is worth real money.

  1. Walk the ground. Find where the water sits, plan the runs and the outlet.
  2. Install on grade. GPS machine control holds the plan through the whole pull.
  3. Hand over the map. The as-built tile map is yours to keep, on paper and as a file.
